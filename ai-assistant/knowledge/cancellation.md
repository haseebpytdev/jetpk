# Cancellation and refund guidance

## General cancellation

Cancellation and change rules depend on the airline fare rules and product type shown at checkout. JetPakistan surfaces supplier/fare conditions — the assistant does not invent refund amounts.

## Refund policy overview

Refund eligibility, timelines, and fees vary by fare brand and supplier. For a specific booking dispute, use Lookup Booking and Contact Support rather than relying on chat guesses.

## Payment timing

Pay within the payment window shown on the booking. Late payment can release the hold. The assistant never processes payments or issues tickets.
