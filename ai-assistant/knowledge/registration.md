# Guest booking and registration

## Guest Booking

Guest Booking lets travellers complete a booking without a full account when the site setting allows it. You still enter passenger and contact details, review the fare, and pay within the stated deadline.

Authentication is always required before Group Booking can proceed. Guest access does not bypass Group eligibility rules.

## Customer Registration

Create a JetPakistan customer account to save travellers, track bookings, and manage payments more easily. Registration uses your email and does not require supplier credentials.

## Saved Travelers

Signed-in customers can store passenger profiles as Saved Travelers to speed up checkout. Ages and document details remain your responsibility to keep accurate.

## Support contact

For booking-specific payment disputes, ticket problems, or document issues, use Contact Support or ask to talk to a human agent in chat.
