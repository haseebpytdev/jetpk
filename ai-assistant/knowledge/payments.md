# Payments help

## Payment deadlines

Each booking shows a payment due time. Unpaid bookings may expire. Card payment availability depends on commerce settings; manual bank transfer may require staff verification.

## Proof of payment

If you paid by bank transfer, upload payment proof from your booking payment page or contact support with the booking reference.

## Refunds

Refunds follow airline and JetPakistan policy for the fare rules on your ticket. Open a support request with your booking reference for refund status.
