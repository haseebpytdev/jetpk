# Groups help

## Group packages

Published group inventory shows sector, airline, departure date, seat availability, and package price. Open a package to review details before booking.

## Searching groups

Use Groups search with origin/destination sector and travel date. Nearby departure dates may appear when an exact date has no seats.

## Booking groups

Group bookings follow the package passenger and payment steps. Seats are limited — confirm availability on the package page before paying.
