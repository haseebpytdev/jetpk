# Booking help

## How booking works

1. Search flights or group packages on JetPakistan.
2. Choose an offer and enter passenger details.
3. Review the fare and payment deadline carefully.
4. Complete payment within the shown window.

Saved travelers can speed up checkout when you are signed in. Guest checkout may be available depending on site settings.

## Manage an existing booking

Use **Lookup Booking** with your booking reference and contact details. For unpaid bookings, complete payment before the deadline shown on the booking.
